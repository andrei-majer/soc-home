Tiers: T0 auto-contain (canary, confirmed-C2) · T1 one-click soc-contain approve <id> · T2 log-only. Detection → off-host actuation, reversible (reaper releases channel bans at TTL; host-isolation released manually). Guardrail assert_not_infra refuses to actuate against lab infrastructure. Instant brake: touch /etc/soc-contain/DISABLED. Armed via soc_contain_dry_run: false + Ansible converge (2026-06-26). Managed-host isolate composes router_isolate (gateway) + vr_quarantine (host-level Velociraptor IPsec L2 lockdown — stops same-subnet lateral movement the gateway drop can't reach on the flat /24); the latter is the newest actuator, staged for its first armed round-trip.
